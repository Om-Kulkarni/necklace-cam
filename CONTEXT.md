# Necklace Cam

A wearable camera that captures moments and delivers them to a phone.

## Language

**Pendant**: The front-worn unit that holds the camera.
_Avoid_: Necklace (when meaning only the front unit)

**Necklace**: The complete wearable, including the Pendant, cord or chain, and Counterweight.

**Counterweight**: The rear unit worn at the back of the neck that holds the battery.

**Capture**: A user-initiated recording of a Clip or Photo.

**Clip**: A short video with optional audio, stored on the device before Transfer.
_Avoid_: Stream

**Photo**: A single still image from a Capture.

**Onboard Storage**: Memory in the Necklace that holds Captures until Transfer.

**Transfer**: Automatic movement of stored Captures to the phone after recording.
_Avoid_: Streaming

**Preview**: A short live view on the phone used for aiming. It is not the saved Capture.

**Recording Indicator**: A light that hardware forces on whenever the Necklace records.
